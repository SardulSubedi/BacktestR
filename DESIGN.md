# BacktestR interface

The accepted direction is a research notebook: a calm, daylight workspace in which the strategy, its assumptions, and the evidence stay beside one another. The interface is an operating tool.

Warm off-white paper, deep green-black ink, fine gray-green rules, and restrained burnt-orange annotations define the world. Georgia headings give research pages a printed character; system sans serves forms and navigation; monospace is reserved for code, tickers, and measurements. No external assets or fonts are required.

The desktop surface has a compact navigation rail, a strategy editor, and a wider report. Mobile stacks the same material in task order. The report has a shared evidence header and tabs for overview, robustness, trades, and code. Empty states explain the next action. Synthetic evidence is prominently labeled at all times.

Clarification is an inline part of editing. The compiled strategy and assumptions are reviewed before running. Edits invalidate old results. Errors retain user input and say what to change. Focus, loading, disabled, empty, and failure states have explicit treatments. Motion is limited to a short report reveal and respects reduced motion.

The notebook uses alignment and shared scales to make experiments comparable, dense legible results tables to make evidence inspectable, and fixed chart axes and legends to avoid decorative data graphics.
